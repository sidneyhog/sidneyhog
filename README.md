## About

I'm Sidney Henrique Oliveira Garcia, and for the past 15+ years I've worked at the intersection of infrastructure, automation and software engineering.

Today I own technology end-to-end for a Brazilian notary office (*cartório*) — not as an outside vendor, but as the person inside the operation who saw manual, error-prone workflows and rebuilt them as software: document automation, internal systems, networking, and the integrations that hold it all together.

I'm also the founder of **NexiaBR**, where I apply the same approach to small and medium businesses: automation architectures, custom-built systems and AI agents designed to solve one specific operational problem at a time — not generic templates.

My work spans infrastructure, DevOps, software engineering and generative AI, not as separate disciplines, but as one practice: designing systems that remove work that shouldn't exist in the first place.

## What I Build

- **Process Automation** — replacing manual, repetitive work with Python scripts, N8N flows and RPA.
- **Infrastructure & DevOps** — containerized environments, CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure that deploys itself.
- **Internal Systems** — software built for how an operation actually works, not for a generic use case.
- **System Integration** — connecting tools that were never designed to talk to each other.
- **AI-Driven Automation** — generative AI applied to decisions and workflows, not just chat interfaces.
